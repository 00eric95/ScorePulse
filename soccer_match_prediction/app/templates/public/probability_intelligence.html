The Problem with Bookmaker Odds

Understanding why bookmaker odds don't represent true probabilities

Overround/Margin

Bookmakers build in a profit margin (typically 8-12%) into their odds. This means the implied probabilities always add up to more than 100%.

105-115%
Typical bookmaker overround

No Football Context

Odds are set by traders focusing on market balance, not football fundamentals. They don't account for team form, injuries, or tactical matchups.

Market-Driven
Not football-driven

The Result

You're betting against odds that are designed to make bookmakers money, not reflect true match probabilities.
